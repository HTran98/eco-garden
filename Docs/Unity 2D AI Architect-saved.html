Traversability Analysis

The level is bisected by a central Pebble Wall [P] which creates a bottleneck. While technically traversable, the limited space around the Producer [S] will lead to rapid grid saturation, forcing the player to engage with grid management mechanics.

Architect's Summary

Level 15 marks a transition from simple merging to "Spatial Puzzling." By restricting the central horizontal axis, we encourage the use of the Shovel tool (monetization hook) or patient gold accumulation for tile expansion.
